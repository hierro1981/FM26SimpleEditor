# Saving policy

At the user's explicit request, saving must remain enabled for readable saves, including unknown current and future game builds. Do not reintroduce a build allowlist or a version-based read-only mode. Keep experimental-build warnings and all existing field, source-fingerprint, archive and reopened-copy integrity checks. Unsupported or ambiguous field layouts must still report their actual decoding or validation error rather than guessing offsets. This policy does not enable financial writes; these require their own verified field handling.
