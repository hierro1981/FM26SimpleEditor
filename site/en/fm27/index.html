Football Manager 27

Your FM27 editor.
A closer look at your career.

From finding a player to choosing your lineup: FM26 Simple Editor now supports Football Manager 27 saves while retaining compatible FM26 features.

Which FM27 version is supported?

Version 16.0.0 supports FM27 build 27.0.0+2493426. The game and save build are identified when opening a file; the check runs again before writing a modified copy.

SaveAvailable actions
FM27 · 27.0.0+2493426View data and edit supported player fields, saving a new copy.
Other FM27 buildsView only, when the format is readable. Editing and saving are disabled.
FM26Existing support remains available, depending on the save format and readable data.

An FM27 update may change the data format. A later build is not automatically enabled just because its number looks similar or its database can be read.

Players, comparison and development

Search by name, club or ID. Combine filters for age, position, current and potential ability, contract and loan status. Edit attributes, CA, PA and positional familiarity; date of birth is editable when read without ambiguity.

The preview shows attributes on the game’s 1–20 scale. Comparison places two players side by side, highlights differences and adds a position report. History lets you view several attributes and compare saves from the same career.

From saved tactics to your best XI

My squad identifies the club of the first recognised human manager. Filter first-team and youth groups, explore candidates by position or use the actual roles from the three saved tactics, when decodable.

The best XI gives each player one slot and seeks the highest overall score. Compare current candidates with estimated future development: this is an editor scenario, not a guarantee of growth or a match-result forecast.

Your backroom team, too

Read jobs, squad assignments, contracts and available attributes. Find staff compares candidates by job or training category; Staff planning helps assess renewals and alternatives, including U20 and U18 vacancies.

Stars and transfer interest are estimates. A strong score does not guarantee that someone will negotiate. Staff data are read only.

What has been checked

An FM26 save and its FM27 conversion were compared: the same 75,687 players and CA/PA values, three matching tactics and 1,115 corresponding internal attribute samples. An updated FM27 save also confirmed all 75 staff names and squad assignments against the supplied game screenshots.

The user also confirmed successful in-game loading of a modified FM27 save. These checks cover the stated build, not every possible career or future update.
