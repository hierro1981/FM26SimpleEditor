Tools for your save

A clearer view of your squad

Open a local save and find the information you need, with tools for exploring players and tactics.

⌕

Search and filters

Search by name, club or ID. Filter by age, role, ability, contract, loan status and other squad data.

✎

Controlled editing

Edit attributes, ability, positions and date of birth when available. Save changes to a new verified copy.

⇄

Comparison and reports

Compare two players and review differences and strengths for a selected position.

♟

My squad

Explore your squad and staff; find candidates for positions and roles from saved tactics.

⌁

Attribute history

View multiple attributes over time and CA on a 1–200 scale from compared saves, with actual dates and changes.

◉

In-game preview

View attributes on the game’s 1–20 scale, positional familiarity and estimated stars.

⌕

Find staff

Search by job or training category. Compare attributes and estimated stars, with interest and U20/U18 destination filters.

▣

Plan renewals

Filter contracts by expiry, job and squad. Compare alternatives with youth-team priority, CA/PA and multi-column sorting.

↗

Prospects and future Best XI

Compare current ability with estimated growth for your tactics. Explore a future Best XI using experimental scenarios, not guaranteed development.

A few simple steps

From save to a new copy

The workflow is local: open a file, review your changes and choose where to save the copy.

OpenA working local copy of your save.
ExploreFind a player or review your squad.
EditCheck your changes in the preview.
Save a copyKeep the original save available.

Compatibility: FM26 and FM27 build 27.0.0+2493426. In-game loading of a modified FM27 save confirmed. Other FM27 builds, when readable, are view-only: editing and saving remain disabled.

Try FM26 Simple Editor

Download the setup for guided installation or the portable ZIP. The editor checks Python and its dependencies at startup. .NET 8 Desktop Runtime x64 is required.
